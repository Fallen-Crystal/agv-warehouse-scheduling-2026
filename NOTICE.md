# Provenance

The domain model, original time-expanded A*, greedy dispatcher, simulation rules, metrics, independent validator and original viewer behavior are derived from the teammate-provided `agv_modeling_shelf` project supplied by the repository owner. The original source directory on the owner's Desktop was preserved. We have not assigned a new license to that third-party contribution.

The owner subsequently supplied the teammate's `agv_modeling_integrated_v4` folder. Its paired-rack geometry, independent shelf-face and layer rules, `2 * shelf_level` loading assumption, delivery-only station rule, expanded fleet/layout controls, completion/stall semantics and weighted evaluation concept were selectively adapted into the existing package. The original flat module tree, duplicate viewer, generated outputs and original report PDF were not imported wholesale. V4 integration, stricter profile/serialization checks, independent layer-order auditing, bounded station-exit retries and V4 experiments are reviewed project-specific adaptations; see `docs/v4-review.md`. This attribution does not grant a new license to the teammate's source.

The package layout, interval planner, reservation index, conflict-based candidate selection, learned candidate ranker, training pipeline, CLI and hardening changes are project-specific adaptations. SIPP, RHCR and MAPF-LNS are prior research; exact papers and inspected open-source repositories are cited in `docs/research/`. External project implementations were inspected as engineering references; they were not vendored into this repository.

Public map data has its own ODC-By attribution notice at `data/NOTICE.md`. Training uses that public source and generated task labels, with no private or undisclosed dataset. The existing ranker has not been retrained or established effective under the V4 profile. V4 benchmark scenes are synthetic, generated from the adapted geometry and task assumptions. Historical v1/v2 and reviewed V4 measurements remain separate in `results/v1/`, `results/v2/` and `results/v4/`.

The owner-supplied `AGV_Report_28pages_Attachment.pdf` was read as research context for the improvement roadmap. Its recommendations are distinguished from implemented behavior, and the PDF itself is not redistributed in this repository.

The owner-supplied BCRD execution plan informed the observation-only ordered-batch dispatch experiment. Its source PDF is not redistributed. The visible shadow, bounded candidate policies and paired experiment tooling are project-specific implementations; they reuse the existing executor and SIPP rather than replacing their safety rules.

The owner later supplied `agv_modeling_mixed_v6` and `agv_modeling_mixed_v9`. V9's mixed horizontal/vertical rack catalog, single-row loading candidates, aligned geometry and clearance metadata are selectively adapted in `warehouse/mixed.py`; V6's useful mixed-rack ideas are included through that latest geometry. The bounded idle controller is adapted from V9's safe return-delay mechanism, replacing its hidden-order termination test with an explicit finite allowance. Explicit legal pickup-side selection is also supported. Original monolithic code, duplicate documents/pages, uncontrolled waits and unsafe generic replanning interfaces were not imported. V9 source tests, geometric identity checks, findings and newly measured same-scene comparisons are documented separately; original V4/BCRD evidence remains intact. No V9 training or real idle-energy model is claimed.
